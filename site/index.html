our story

Baked the way it should be

The Baker's Pantry is a boutique dairy bakery in the heart of Monroe, New York. We believe cheese desserts deserve the same artistry as fine patisserie — real cheese fillings, buttery doughs, and finishing touches piped and placed entirely by hand.

From a single cheese floret to a towering babka, everything is baked in small batches so it reaches your table tasting the way it did coming out of our oven.
