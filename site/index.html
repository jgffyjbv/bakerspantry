Our family bakehouse in Monroe crafts fresh, mouthwatering dairy confections for people who can taste the difference. Every cheesecake is baked slow, every babka piped high with real cream, and nothing leaves the kitchen until it looks as good as it tastes.

What sets a Baker's Pantry cake apart is the handwork — the piping, the glazing, the finishing touches placed one by one. The trends change, but at the core there is always a solidly delectable cake.

One of our biggest secrets is simple: the freshest, most authentic ingredients, never substituted with cheaper ready-made shortcuts. That is where the delicate, signature flavor comes from — and why the first bite tastes like it came straight from the oven.

— The Baker's Pantry, Monroe NY
