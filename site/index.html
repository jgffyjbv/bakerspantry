Our Story

More Than a Bakery

The Baker's Pantry was born from a love of true dairy baking — the kind where a cheesecake is baked slow, a babka is piped high with real cream, and nothing leaves the kitchen until it looks as good as it tastes.

From our home in Monroe, New York, we bake in small batches and finish every piece by hand — for your Shabbos table, your simcha, and every sweet occasion in between.
