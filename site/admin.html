Add a Product

New products show up on the shop right away, and on the front page under their category. Occasions power the "Filter by occasion" dropdown on the shop — tick Milchig/Pareve and any occasions the item fits.

Occasions & Filters

These show up in the "Filter by occasion" dropdown on the shop page. Tag your products with them in the Products tab. Customers only see an occasion once at least one product is tagged with it.

Add a Party Planner

Party planners get their own sign-in on the shop page ("Party Planner Login" in the menu). When they're signed in, their discount comes off automatically at checkout and their name shows on the order.

Send the planner their email + password yourself (WhatsApp / phone). You can change the discount or password here any time — it takes effect on their next order.

Order Notifications

Every new order is saved here in the Orders tab. To ALSO get an email for each new order, enter the email address below.

One-time step: order emails come from orders@bhwebs.com. The first time you use a new address, Cloudflare sends it a one-time verification email — open it and click the link once, and all future order emails come through automatically.
